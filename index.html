Epic Lifestyle Center

Where a city rehearses its future.

Part cultural institution. Part human-performance lab. Part urban living room.

EPIC Lifestyle Center is Colombo's next-generation life hub: a vertical landscape where architecture, wellness, media, gastronomy, and markets are choreographed under one roof. Think: if a museum of modern art, a fashion house, and a science lab decided to build a home together-and left the door open for the city.

This is not just a building. It's an operating system for how we gather, recover, create, and play.

About

A monument to what life could look like.

EPIC is designed as a soft machine: light, sound, circulation, and space all tuned for human experience. Mornings feel like a studio residency; afternoons, like a think tank; nights, like a curated festival.

Every zone is modular, elastic, and data-aware-built to host everything from global summits to silent retreats, film premieres to wellness rituals. The architecture doesn't just hold events; it amplifies them.

Why EPIC, Why Now?

Cities are evolving from collections of buildings into networks of experiences. EPIC Lifestyle Center is built as a prototype of that future: one address, many interfaces.

  • Events produce culture.
  • Wellness and biohacking produce resilience.
  • Studios produce narrative.
  • Cafes and markets produce community.

The result is an ecosystem where human potential isn't just talked about on a stage-it's tested, trained, filmed, and shared.

EPIC GLASS HOUSE

Liquid Light Observatory

A suspended lounge made from layered glass, mirrored steel, and vapor-light projections. Guests step inside a floating atrium where reflections, aromas, and spatial sound shift with the time of day.

  • Morning: sound baths and sunrise strategy sessions.
  • Afternoon: product unveilings on a translucent runway.
  • Night: immersive dinners with reactive lighting and scent.
